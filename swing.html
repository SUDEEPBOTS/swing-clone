The First Class

You arrive as you are. There is no fitness test, no intake form asking what you used to lift. The first class is forty minutes: ten minutes of breath and movement to arrive, twenty minutes of foundational swing work, ten minutes of rest and stillness. You leave knowing the difference between tension you chose and tension that chose you.

“My shoulders had been locked for two years. Three months in, I forgot they were ever a problem.”

Tariq O. — Software engineer, remote

Iron and Breath

Kettlebell training is a conversation between effort and recovery. Every swing teaches you to generate force and then release it completely — a practice that transfers off the studio floor. We program rest as deliberately as we program load. The bell goes down. The breath comes in. This is the work.

“We trade off childcare to get here. Twenty minutes of being just ourselves again. It's enough. It's everything.”

Rosa & Emeka F. — New parents, 8 months in

Who This Is For

Burned-out nurses finishing night shifts. Desk-bound developers with frozen shoulders. New parents reclaiming twenty minutes of sovereignty over their own bodies. Retirees who refused the rocking chair. The class is not sorted by fitness level. It is sorted by presence — everyone who shows up is exactly qualified.

“I told myself I was past it. Swing proved me wrong. I swing heavier now than I did at fifty.”

Leonard K. — Retired architect, 68

Four formats.
One floor.

Every class shares the same philosophy: you are not here to be broken down. You are here to be built up — carefully, over time, with attention.

Restore

40 min
Restore

Breath-led movement, joint mobility, and light bell work. The class for arriving as you are.

Nasal breathing throughout. No heart rate spikes. You leave lighter than you arrived.

Post-shift, post-illness, first-timers, and anyone who needs to move without proving anything.

Flow

50 min
Flow

Rhythmic swing sequences that build over the hour. The core of the Swing practice.

Two-handed swings, single-arm progressions, and structured rest intervals. Leaves you tired in the good way.

Members with 2+ weeks of foundation. The workhorse class.

Forge

55 min
Forge

Heavier bells, complex movement patterns, and longer work sets. For the committed regular.

Turkish get-ups, cleans, presses, and loaded carries. Technical coaching throughout.

Members who have completed at least 8 Flow sessions. Not a beginner class.

Open Bell

60 min
Flow

Unstructured practice time with a coach present. Work on what you want to work on.

Coaches circulate, offer feedback, and leave you alone when that's what you need.

All levels. Bring a question or just come to move.
